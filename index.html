Scoring

Ask just 1 question per suspect and name the liar for a perfect 100/100. Every extra question lowers your score by about 7 points.

A hint costs as much as one question per suspect (20 points). A wrong accusation fails the case: 0/100, and your streak resets. You need at least 2 questions before you can accuse.

Stress meter: every suspect has one. Friendly, calm questions lower it. Sharp questions, threats, and facts that expose a lie raise it, and a liar’s stress climbs a little faster. Push someone too far and they confess, even if they’re innocent. A false confession won’t save you, so pick wisely.

Questions only: you can only talk, with no actions. Keep each question to 50 words or fewer and ask one thing at a time. The mood emoji next to each name shows how they’re feeling, from 😊 calm to 😨 panicking.
